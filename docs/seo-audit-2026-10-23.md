# Discovery audit and implementation evidence

Scope: the bilingual personal portfolio, experience, services, résumé, projects, and technical writing. The conversion goal is senior software engineering employment, technology leadership opportunities, and independent software projects. This is a source/build/browser audit, not Search Console access or a guarantee of rankings.

## Executive summary

The site already has static HTML, self-canonical locale routes, visibility filtering, a connected Person/WebSite/WebPage graph, sitemap discovery, and crawlable content links. Preserve those foundations. The highest-value corrections are broken Search lifecycle after navigation, missing social cards, translated article alternate availability, and absent article-series navigation.

## Preservation and limits

- Root English and `/es/` Spanish stay distinct, self-canonical, and linked only to available counterparts. Hidden/draft entries remain excluded from discovery and series links.
- Keep `ProfilePage`/`Person` for a professional profile. A candidate seeking work is not a `JobPosting`; no job, title, certification, employer, or review is invented.
- Series links are ordinary HTML anchors, not JavaScript-only controls. Standalone posts need no new metadata.
- Social images are generated at build and served by Workers Static Assets. They do not add request-time image CPU, AI services, or a new remote dependency.
- Public crawling is intentionally enabled while admin/API remain protected. Robots directives are not authorization. Search remains noindex.
- No personal ignored Markdown, private resource configuration, telemetry/auth contract, deployment, account settings, or production data is changed by this feature.
- The optional IndexNow publisher is not proof that a search engine accepted or indexed a URL. No submissions were performed.

## Verification

Feature-level observations and remaining checks are recorded in [the task log](../odd/tasks/discovery-content-upgrade.md). Verify generated HTML, JSON-LD in the browser, sitemap/HTML alternate agreement, actual image bytes and dimensions, hidden-content exclusions, responsive controls, repeated navigation and both locales. Tests with synthetic series do not claim that private editorial content has been assigned to a series.

Search Console coverage, real query positions, conversion rates, field LCP/INP/CLS, provider settings and search-engine acceptance require separate authorized evidence. They remain unmeasured here; no performance or ranking score is inferred from architecture.
