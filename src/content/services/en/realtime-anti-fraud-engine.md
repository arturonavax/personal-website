### Overview

In transactional processing, every millisecond counts. A slow risk engine degrades checkout conversion, while permissive heuristics expose platforms to chargebacks, direct financial loss, and collusion. I build deterministic, high-concurrency evaluation engines designed to calculate velocity metrics and enforce security rules in under 50 milliseconds before authorizing high-value transactions.

### What We Deliver

1. **Sub-50ms Synchronous Latency**: Frictionless inline risk evaluation imperceptible to legitimate customers during checkout and payment processing.
2. **Dynamic Risk Orchestration**: Instant threshold and heuristic adjustments to counter emerging attack vectors without redeploying code.
3. **Collusion & Abuse Mitigation**: Automated detection of cashier collusion, velocity anomalies, and reward abuse across point-of-sale systems.
4. **Cryptographic Identity Defense**: High-throughput verification pipelines backed by failover-safe relational storage and rate-limiting safeguards.
