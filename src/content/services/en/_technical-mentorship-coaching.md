### Overview

Reaching the Senior, Staff, or Principal Engineer tier requires a fundamental shift from writing code to navigating ambiguous organizational requirements, modeling trade-offs, and driving sound technical consensus across multi-disciplinary teams.

### What We Work On

- **System Design Mastery**: Deconstructing high-scale real-world systems (Uber dispatch, Netflix streaming, Kafka storage engines, Stripe ledger reconciliation).
- **Concurrency & Memory Primitives**: Deep mastery of Go memory models, atomic operations, sync pools, lock-free queues, and cache-line friendliness.
- **Architectural Communication**: Writing compelling RFCs, ADRs, and technical proposals that gain executive and engineering buy-in.
- **Career Strategy**: Identifying high-leverage organizational initiatives, building technical reputation, and preparing for top-tier Staff interviews.
