### Overview

Enterprise operational knowledge is frequently trapped in siloed repositories and legacy document stores. Deploying an enterprise-grade RAG engine demands far more than naive document-to-LLM pipelines; it requires advanced semantic chunking, low-latency hybrid retrieval, and deterministic access governance to guarantee employees only retrieve records permitted by their operational role.

### What We Deliver

1. **High-Precision Semantic Retrieval**: Sub-second location of internal compliance policies, contract clauses, and complex technical system specifications.
2. **Verifiable Source Attribution**: Every synthesized answer is explicitly grounded with direct citations, line extracts, and links to source documents.
3. **Privilege & Tenant Isolation**: Native enforcement of enterprise permission boundaries paired with tamper-evident query audit logging.
4. **Latency & Cost Optimization**: Distributed semantic caching layers that accelerate frequent queries and reduce inference API spend.
