### Overview

In Web3 and decentralized finance, deployed code is immutable and custodies capital directly; any flaw in mathematical invariants or access controls results in irrevocable financial loss. I build smart contracts and decentralized protocols grounded in defensive security engineering, formal invariant modeling, and optimal on-chain resource consumption.

### What We Deliver

1. **Deterministic Financial Primitives**: Mathematical modeling of automated liquidity pools, lending markets, staking mechanisms, programmatic distributions, and liquidation engines.
2. **Economic Attack Mitigation**: Structural defense mechanisms against flash-loan liquidity draining, sandwich attacks, and price oracle manipulation.
3. **On-Chain Compute Efficiency**: Data type packing, storage layout reorganizations, and execution optimizations to ensure accessible, predictable network fees.
4. **Traceability & Formal Verification**: Comprehensive technical specifications and automated property testing proving protocol invariants remain unbroken across adversarial execution states.
